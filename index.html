ABOUT US

Precision, Workmanship, and Elegance

AL HIJARAH AL FAREEDAH PLASTER & TILES CONT. CO. L.L.C, 20 years expertise in interior fit-out and gypsum contracting, we have been transforming empty spaces into functional and visually stunning environments. Our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to delivering exceptional results that exceed client expectations.

We are specialized in providing top-notch plastering, tiling, and interior fit out services. Having years of experience in the field, our experts guarantee not only strength but also elegant details of your interior design.

Delivers specialized gypsum plasterboard solutions and luxury interior fit-outs for commercial offices, residential high-rises, and public facilities. From custom suspended ceilings with integrated cove lighting to high-performance soundproof partition walls, we combine acoustic engineering with sharp aesthetic craftsmanship.

Operating under strict Municipality and Civil Defence standards, our certified site engineers manage every stage from sub-frame structural calculation to final micro-finish painting.

WHAT WE DO

Tailored Interior & Gypsum Solutions

01

Fit-Out Services

Entire end-to-end interior fit out services according to your floor plan. We create partition walls, ceiling installation, flooring, and special architectural elements that will help you make use of your space to the best.

02

Tile Supply & Installation

Fitting porcelain, ceramic, marble, mosaic and natural stone tiles with precision. We provide proper sub-floor preparation, alignment, waterproof grouting, and edge alignment for bathrooms, kitchens, and traffic-heavy floors.

03

Gypsum Work and Painting

We offer top-quality gypsum partition work, false ceiling, designer ceiling work and Interior/Exterior Painting. We have a skilled team that offers smooth wall finish, designer ceilings with LED lighting, and excellent painting using premium quality paints.

04

Showroom Fit-Outs

High-impact interior fit outs for your product presentation. Our specialists will develop the floor plan, perform high-traffic tiling, install dynamic lighting system and wall finishing.

05

Office Renovations

Special fit-out solutions that will increase productivity and enhance the look of your office. We provide acoustical ceiling panels, durable flooring, partitions, modern paint finishes.

06

Home Decoration & Styling

Interior renovation of your house with accent feature walls, custom plaster design, fireplace surround, decorative wall paneling and luxury residential tiling.

07

Interior & Exterior Painting

Professional surface preparation, priming and painting with quality paints. We also offer texture application, matte/gloss paint finish, protective exterior paint, custom paint color mixing.

08

Glass Work

Custom glass installations, including partitions, doors, and decorative elements, designed to enhance the aesthetics and functionality of your space.
